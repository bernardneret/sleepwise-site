SleepWise Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

The short version: your sleep data stays on your phone. SleepWise reads sleep data from Apple Health, scores it on your device, and stores it in a local database inside the app. We never upload your sleep or health data anywhere. The only information that leaves your device is a small set of anonymous usage analytics that contain no health information, plus the delivery details you choose to give us when you redeem a reward. The full details are below.

1. Introduction and scope

SleepWise is an iOS application operated by Bernard Neret ("SleepWise", "we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the SleepWise app, join our waitlist, or contact us. It applies to the SleepWise app distributed through Apple TestFlight during our beta program and, when released, through the Apple App Store.

By installing or using SleepWise, you acknowledge the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, do not install or use the app.

Contact for all privacy matters: neretb56@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Health data from Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your explicit permission, granted through Apple's HealthKit permission system, SleepWise reads the following categories of data from Apple Health:

  • Sleep analysis data: sleep sessions and sleep stages (deep, core/light, REM, and awake periods), including their start times, end times, and durations;
  • Heart rate samples recorded during your sleep window, which the app summarizes (for example, into a nightly average, low, and high, and a simplified overnight curve) for scoring and display;
  • Heart rate variability (HRV) samples recorded during your sleep window, which the app summarizes into a nightly average used as a recovery input to your sleep score.

These three categories (sleep analysis, heart rate, and heart rate variability) are the only Apple Health data types the app requests or reads.

This data originates from your wearable device (such as Apple Watch, Garmin, Oura, Whoop, or Fitbit) or other apps that write sleep data to Apple Health. SleepWise does not itself measure your sleep.

Where this data lives: all health data read by SleepWise is processed and stored exclusively on your device, in a local database inside the app's private storage container. It is never transmitted to us, to our service providers, or to any other party. There is no SleepWise server that holds your health data, and no account to which it is linked.

Read-only: SleepWise requests read-only access. The app does not write, modify, or delete any data in Apple Health.

When you first grant access (and if you use the app's history-reconstruction feature), SleepWise may read historical sleep data from Apple Health covering a look-back period, in order to show your history and compute scores. This historical data is subject to the same rule: on-device only, never transmitted.

2.2 Data generated by the app on your device

SleepWise computes and stores the following on your device only: nightly sleep scores and sub-scores, coin balances and coin transaction history, streaks and challenge progress, reward redemption records, your settings (such as sleep-need target and notification preferences), and internal logs used for the app's own operation. None of this is transmitted to us, with two exceptions: the anonymous analytics events described in Section 2.3, which never include scores, health values, or any health-derived numbers, and the delivery details you provide when you redeem a reward, described in Section 2.4.

2.3 Anonymous usage analytics

To understand whether the app works, where onboarding fails, and whether it gets used, we collect a limited set of anonymous usage events using PostHog, Inc. ("PostHog"), an analytics service with servers located in the United States. The events are limited to app-usage signals such as:

  • the app was installed (first launch);
  • Apple Health access was granted (recorded as a yes/no funnel step; no health data is included);
  • a night of sleep data was synced, together with the calendar date of the night it covered (never the content of the data: no durations, stages, scores, or heart measurements);
  • the first night ever synced on an install (a one-time "activated" milestone with no further detail);
  • setup was completed (a one-time funnel step recording that you reached the main app);
  • the app was opened on a given day;
  • a screen or tab was viewed (for example, Sleep, Coach, or Rewards);
  • a morning report was viewed;
  • a morning-report notification was scheduled or presented (the fact that the day's ping was queued, and whether it was shown while the app was running; we use this to check that background delivery is working);
  • a fallback reminder fired because the morning report had not yet been viewed (the fact of the reminder only);
  • notification preferences were chosen (which toggles, the outcome of the iOS permission prompt, and whether a later re-ask was accepted);
  • a reward was redeemed (which reward from our fixed catalog, its listed coin price, and the random redemption identifier described in Section 2.4, which contains no personal information).

These events are associated with:

  • a random install identifier generated on your device at first launch. It is not derived from your name, email, phone number, Apple ID, device serial number, advertising identifier, or any other personal identifier, and we do not use it to identify you. If you redeem a reward, the random redemption identifier (Section 2.4) lets us match that one redemption event to the delivery details you gave us; we use this only to fulfill and account for the reward, never to connect your other analytics events to you;
  • a source tag indicating the recruiting channel through which you found SleepWise (for example, waitlist or Reddit), where applicable;
  • a build/variant tag used to separate our own test devices from real testers, plus the app's build number and app-code update identifier, so we can tell which version of the app produced an event. These same tags are stored in PostHog as properties of the install identifier;
  • event timestamps;
  • the event-specific details noted in the list above (for example, which tab, which toggles, or which reward), and nothing more.

What analytics never contain: sleep scores, sleep stages, sleep durations, heart rate, heart rate variability, coin balances derived from your sleep, or any other health measurement or health-derived value. The closest an analytics event comes to your sleep is the calendar date attached to a synced-night event, and a reward's coin price is its fixed catalog price, not your balance. This exclusion is deliberate and enforced in how the app is built.

IP addresses: as with any transmission over the internet, PostHog's servers receive your device's IP address when analytics events are delivered. We do not use IP addresses to identify you. PostHog's processing of data on our behalf is governed by its own terms and privacy documentation, available at posthog.com.

2.4 Reward fulfillment

When you redeem a reward, we ask for the details needed to deliver it: an email address for digital rewards such as gift cards, and your name and a US shipping address for physical items. You provide these only at the moment you redeem, and only for the item you are redeeming.

These details are sent to Formspree, a form-processing service we use to receive redemption requests, and delivered to us by email. We use them solely to fulfill your reward. They are not combined with your sleep data, are not sent to our analytics provider, and are not sold or shared for any other purpose. Each redemption carries a random identifier so we can match the request to the reward; that identifier contains no personal information.

We delete redemption details from our records within 90 days of fulfilling the reward. Gift cards are delivered by email from the card provider (for example, Amazon), whose own terms apply to the card once delivered.

2.5 Waitlist and email signups

If you join our waitlist or sign up for updates on the web, we collect your email address through Formspree, Inc. ("Formspree"), a form-processing service. We use it solely to contact you about SleepWise availability, beta invitations, and launch updates. Waitlist emails are stored with Formspree and in our email records. They are not connected to the app, to your install identifier, or to analytics in any way. You may request removal at any time by emailing neretb56@gmail.com.

2.6 Information you send us directly

If you email us, respond to a beta survey, or participate in a feedback interview, we receive whatever information you choose to share (such as your email address and the content of your message). We use it only to respond to you and to improve SleepWise. Feedback may be retained as part of our development records.

2.7 TestFlight beta data collected by Apple

During the beta, SleepWise is distributed through Apple's TestFlight. Apple collects certain information from beta testers under its own terms, which may include your email address (used for the invitation), device type, iOS version, crash logs, and basic usage statistics, and shares some of this with us in aggregate or diagnostic form (for example, crash reports and session counts). Apple's collection is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and the TestFlight terms, not by this Policy. Crash logs we receive through Apple do not contain your health data.

2.8 App update delivery (Expo)

The app keeps itself up to date between App Store or TestFlight releases by periodically checking for updated app code using EAS Update, a service operated by Expo (650 Industries, Inc., "Expo"). When the app checks for or downloads an update, Expo's servers receive standard request data (such as your IP address) together with technical update metadata: the app's runtime version, platform, update channel, and a randomly generated client identifier used to deliver updates and count how many devices received one. These requests contain no health data, no analytics events, and nothing linked to your analytics install identifier. Expo's processing is governed by its own privacy policy.

2.9 Information we do not collect

  • No accounts: the app has no registration, login, or profile, and does not ask for your email address, phone number, or contact details, except the delivery details you provide when you redeem a reward (Section 2.4). During setup you may optionally enter a first name and your age; both stay on your device, are used only to tailor what the app shows you, and are never transmitted.
  • No precise location data.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA) and no ad-tracking SDKs.
  • No sale of data of any kind, and no sharing of data with data brokers.

3. HealthKit-specific commitments

In accordance with Apple's developer requirements and our own policy:

  • We do not use information gained through HealthKit for advertising, marketing, or other use-based data mining;
  • We do not sell information gained through HealthKit to advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers;
  • We do not disclose HealthKit information to any third party (a disclosure obligation that is currently easy to honor, because HealthKit data never leaves your device);
  • We use HealthKit data solely to provide the app's sleep-tracking, scoring, insight, and rewards features to you, on your device.

4. How we use information

  • Health data (on-device): to compute your nightly sleep scores, display your sleep history and trends, generate coaching insights and morning reports, and determine coin earnings, all locally on your device.
  • Anonymous analytics: to measure onboarding completion, activation, retention, feature usage, and redemption activity in aggregate; to find and fix problems; and to make product decisions.
  • Reward fulfillment details: contact details (email, name, shipping address) when you redeem a reward, used only to deliver that reward.
  • Waitlist emails: to send availability, beta, and launch communications.
  • Direct communications: to respond to you and support your use of the app.

We do not use any of the above for third-party advertising, and we do not build advertising or marketing profiles of users.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. Sharing is limited to the following:

  • Service providers (processors): PostHog (anonymous analytics hosting) and Formspree (form processing for waitlist signups and for reward redemption requests, which carry the delivery details described in Section 2.4) process limited data on our behalf as described above. GitHub, Inc. hosts this policy page and, like any web host, receives standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) when you visit it. Expo (650 Industries, Inc.) delivers app code updates and receives the technical update metadata described in Section 2.8. Apple provides TestFlight distribution and crash reporting. These providers are bound by their own terms and are used only for the purposes described in this Policy.

Note that because health data exists only on your device, we have no ability to produce it in response to any such request.
  • Business transfers: if SleepWise is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, formation of a legal entity, or sale of assets, the limited information we hold (such as waitlist emails and anonymous analytics) may be transferred as part of that transaction. 6. Rewards and third parties

Rewards in the current catalog are digital gift cards delivered by email and physical sleep accessories shipped to you. To deliver a gift card, we purchase it from the card provider (for example, Amazon) and have it sent to the email address you gave us at redemption; the provider's own privacy policy and terms govern the card once it is delivered. To ship a physical item, we give your name and shipping address to the retailer we order from and to the shipping carrier, solely for delivery. We do not share your sleep data, your install identifier, or anything else with these parties, and a redemption event recorded in our analytics reflects which catalog reward was redeemed and its listed coin price, not who you are and nothing about your sleep.

If the catalog ever includes a reward that opens a link to a third-party retailer's website, the retailer's own privacy policy and terms govern any information you provide to them once you leave SleepWise. We do not currently use affiliate links; if we do in the future, any commission would not affect the price you pay and would not involve any exchange of your personal information.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Health and app data: stored on your device for as long as the app is installed. Deleting the app permanently deletes the app's local database, including all sleep history, scores, and coin records held by SleepWise. Your underlying data remains in Apple Health under Apple's controls, and you can manage or delete it there.
  • Anonymous analytics: retained in PostHog for as long as reasonably needed for product analysis. Because events are keyed only to a random install identifier, they cannot be traced back to you, and deleting the app ends all further collection from your device.
  • Reward redemption details: deleted from our records within 90 days of fulfilling the reward.
  • Waitlist emails: retained until launch communications conclude or you request removal, whichever comes first.
  • Correspondence: retained as long as needed for support and product development records.

8. Security

Your health data benefits from the strongest protection available to us: it never leaves your device. On-device data is protected by iOS's application sandboxing and your device's own security (passcode, Face ID/Touch ID, and hardware encryption). Analytics transmissions to PostHog are encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to our analytics and waitlist accounts is restricted and credential-protected.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the unlikely event of a breach of data we hold that triggers notification obligations (including under the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule or applicable state law), we will notify affected individuals and regulators as required by law.

9. Your choices and rights

9.1 Controls available to everyone

  • Health access: you can revoke SleepWise's access to Apple Health at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Health (or in the Health app under Sharing). The app will stop reading new data immediately.
  • Notifications: controlled in the app's settings and in iOS Settings > Notifications.
  • Analytics: collection is tied to the app; deleting the app stops it entirely. If you would like your anonymous analytics excluded from our analysis, contact us and we will make reasonable efforts to honor it.
  • Deletion: deleting the app deletes all data SleepWise holds on your device. For waitlist or correspondence data, email us and we will delete it.
  • Access and correction: you can email us to ask what information we hold about you (for most users: at most a waitlist email address or the delivery details from a reward redemption) and to correct or delete it.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes requiring a right to limit. SleepWise's design means we do not collect, share, or sell consumer health data off your device: sleep, heart-rate, and heart-rate-variability data are processed and stored on your device only. To the extent any information we do hold (such as anonymous analytics reflecting that a sync occurred) is deemed consumer health data under these laws, you have the right to confirm whether we collect it, to access it, to have it deleted, and to withdraw consent, and we do not sell it or share it for advertising. Requests: neretb56@gmail.com. If your request is denied, you may appeal by replying to our decision, and we will review the appeal within the timeframe required by your state's law.
